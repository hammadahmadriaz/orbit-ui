# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

Everyone is welcome in the Orbit UI community, whatever their background, experience level or identity. We want this project to be a friendly place to learn and build.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that the maintainer has the final say on what goes into the project.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Publishing someone's private information
- Spam and self promotion in issues

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and block accounts that break these rules. Report problems to hammadahmadriaz@gmail.com. Reports are kept private.

This code is based on the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
